Ella theme setup: the checklist I follow

05 Sep 2026 · My step-by-step order for setting up the Ella Shopify theme, so you don't redo work later.

I set up a full store on the Ella theme. The first time, I did things in the wrong order and redid half of it.

This is the order I use now.

1. Store basics first

  • Store name, currency and time zone.
  • Shipping zones and rates.
  • Payment providers.
  • Policy pages: refund, privacy, terms, shipping.

2. Plan collections before products

Write down your collections on paper. Menus, filters and homepage blocks all pull from them.

Use automated collections where you can. Tag products, and the collection fills itself.

3. Add products

  • Clear titles. No keyword stuffing.
  • Variants for size and colour.
  • Square images at the same size, so grids line up.
  • Tags that match your collection rules.

4. Install and duplicate the theme

Always work on a duplicate. Keep the published theme as your backup.

5. Build the homepage

Ella has many section types. Use fewer than you think. Hero, featured collections, a trust bar, reviews and a newsletter is plenty.

6. Menus and CMS pages

Main menu, footer menu, then About, Contact and FAQ pages.

7. Check on a real phone

Most orders come from mobile. Tap every button. Try the cart and checkout.

Takeaway

Collections first. Duplicate theme always. Test on mobile last and again after every change.
