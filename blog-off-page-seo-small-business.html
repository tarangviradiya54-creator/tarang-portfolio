Off-page SEO for small businesses

21 Sep 2026 · What I learned doing off-page SEO at an agency. What works, what wastes time.

I spent four months doing off-page SEO at a digital agency. Here's what I'd tell a small business owner.

What off-page SEO is

Everything outside your site that tells Google you're trustworthy. Mostly links and mentions.

What worked

  • Business listings. Google Business Profile first. Then trusted local directories. Same name, address and phone everywhere.
  • Relevant directories. Industry sites beat random ones.
  • Profiles on real platforms. Social profiles and professional sites that link back.

What wasted time

  • Hundreds of low-quality bookmarking links.
  • Directories nobody visits.
  • Anything that promises "1000 backlinks for $10".

Keyword research first

Before building links, pick what you want to rank for. Look at what competitors rank for. Find the gaps.

Be patient

Off-page SEO shows results over months. Track rankings monthly, not daily.
