Shopify store on the Ella theme

Shopify · Liquid Full setup of a paid theme: products, collections, menus and CMS pages.

The goal

Take a paid Shopify theme from a fresh install to a store that looks ready to sell.

What I did

  • Installed and configured the Ella theme.
  • Added products with variants, images and descriptions.
  • Planned and built collections before adding products to menus.
  • Set up the main menu, footer menu and mega menu.
  • Built CMS pages: About, Contact, FAQ and policy pages.
  • Adjusted sections and styling in Liquid and CSS.

What I learned

Collections come first. Filters, menus and the homepage all depend on them.

I wrote the full order down in my Ella setup checklist.
