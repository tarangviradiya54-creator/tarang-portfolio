About me

I'm Tarang. I build websites and I like fixing broken ones.

I finished my BCA in 2026 at LJ Institute of Computer Application, Ahmedabad.

College taught me the basics. The rest I learned by building.

I started with HTML and CSS. Then core PHP and MySQL. Then WordPress, because so much of the web runs on it.

After that came Shopify. I set up a full store on the paid Ella theme and learned Liquid along the way.

Agency experience

From January to April 2026 I worked as an SEO intern at Cubic Digital.

I built backlinks, ran keyword research and studied competitors for client sites.

It taught me how search really works. Now I build sites with that in mind from day one.

How I work

  • I ask questions early. It saves rework later.
